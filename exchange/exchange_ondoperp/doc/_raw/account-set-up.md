# Account Setup

Ondo Perps supports logins through web3 wallets or email. Create your account at [https://app.ondoperps.xyz/](https://app.ondoperps.xyz/) and deposit collateral to begin trading.

### **Supported Wallets**

Ondo Perps supports a range of self-custody wallets. Connect using any of the following:

* Coinbase Wallet
* Brave Wallet
* Phantom
* MetaMask
* OKX Wallet
* Nightly
* WalletConnect-compatible wallets

**Eligibility**

Ondo Perps is available globally to eligible users outside the U.S., Panama and other prohibited jurisdictions. Persons and residents of the U.S. and other prohibited jurisdictions are not eligible to use the platform. For more information, review our Terms of Service.
