# Perpetual Futures

Perpetual futures provide continuous, leveraged exposure to an underlying market, such as stocks or commodities. Unlike traditional futures, perpetuals have no expiry date and require no rolling; positions can be held indefinitely with sufficient margin, with funding rates keeping perp prices anchored to the underlying asset.

Ondo Perps offers perpetual futures on the world's most actively traded U.S. equities, indices, ETFs, and commodities. Trading is available 24/7, including outside traditional market hours and on weekends.

Key characteristics:

* No expiry date
* Long and short positions supported
* 24/7 trading availability
* Funding rates are designed to keep perp prices anchored to the underlying asset
* Positions can be held indefinitely with sufficient margin
